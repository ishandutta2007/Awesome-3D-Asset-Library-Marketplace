# Awesome-3D-Asset-Library-Marketplace

## Top 3D Asset Library & Marketplace Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on CC0 Asset Repositories, Integrated Library Plugins & Self-Hosted Marketplaces*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **3D Asset Libraries & Marketplaces**. These tools and services provide artists, game developers, and VFX professionals with ready-to-use 3D models, textures, HDRIs, and materials—ranging from commercial marketplaces with millions of assets to community-driven CC0 libraries and self-hosted marketplace platforms.



**Examples** include Adobe Substance 3D Assets, Quixel Megascans, Poliigon, TurboSquid, Sketchfab, CGTrader, KitBash3D, ArtStation Marketplace, Poly Haven, and BlenderKit (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, avoiding subscription fees, and building custom asset distribution platforms. The open-source landscape is anchored by **Poly Haven** (the CC0 quality standard with no accounts or paywalls), **BlenderKit** (the community-driven library integrated directly into Blender), and **Icosa Gallery** (the open-source successor to Google Poly), with **3D RGB** and **3Dēx** providing full marketplace platforms for those wanting to operate their own storefronts.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)** [![GitHub Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/polyhaven?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/polyhaven)  

  **The CC0 quality standard.** A curated public library of HDRIs (16K+ resolution), PBR textures (8K+), and 3D models—all **CC0 licensed**, meaning no copyright restrictions and usable for any purpose . **No paywalls, no accounts, no email forms**—just download and use . **Funding**: Patreon donations and corporate sponsors; transparent financial reports published . Best for artists and developers wanting high-quality assets without licensing complexity or cost.



- **[BlenderKit](https://github.com/BlenderKit)**   

  **The community-driven library integrated into Blender.** Free to use with a **Full Plan subscription** supporting creators and open-source development . Provides an ever-growing asset library directly accessible within Blender's interface—search, download, and drag assets into scenes without leaving the 3D viewport. **Ecosystem**: add-ons for Godot, Maya, and Unreal Engine available on GitHub under GPL licenses . Best for Blender users wanting seamless in-app asset access with optional creator support.



- **[Icosa Gallery](https://github.com/icosa-foundation/icosa-gallery)** [![GitHub Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/icosa-foundation/icosa-gallery?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/icosa-foundation/icosa-gallery/stargazers)  

  **The open-source successor to Google Poly.** Apache 2.0 licensed, ~31 stars, actively maintained (last push September 2026) . A 3D model hosting platform for sharing, viewing, and distributing 3D assets—part of the Icosa Foundation ecosystem alongside **Open Brush** (3D painting) and **Open Blocks** (low-poly modeling) . **Goals**: improved format conversion, Blender and Godot integrations, and enhanced reusability for embedding in websites . Best for developers wanting an open, community-led platform for hosting and distributing 3D content.



- **[3D RGB (3DMeta-Shop)](https://github.com/iranpsc/3DMeta-Shop-Object-3D)**   

  **The self-hosted marketplace platform.** Full-featured digital marketplace for selling 3D models, avatars, textures, and renders—built with **Laravel, Blade, and Tailwind CSS** . **Features**: user registration, product listing with search/filtering, shopping cart, secure checkout, download management, admin panel with product/user/order management, and role-based access control . **Roadmap**: payment gateway integration, multi-vendor support, ratings/reviews, and 3D model preview viewer . Best for artists or studios wanting to operate their own 3D asset storefront without marketplace fees.



- **[3Dēx](https://github.com/Schryzon/3Dex)**   

  **The AI-powered marketplace with physical print integration.** Multi-sided marketplace connecting creators, consumers, and manufacturers . **Features**: interactive 3D previews in browser, advanced search, community feed, content moderation with NSFW filtering, artist analytics dashboards, and **AI-powered discovery** (semantic search via pgvector + MiniLM, contextual assistant via Gemini) . **Business model**: 85% artist payout on digital sales, 75% provider payout on physical prints . **Tech stack**: Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, MinIO . Best for teams wanting a modern, feature-rich marketplace with both digital and physical asset commerce.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **open-source-3D-assets** — CC0 3D asset registry with 991+ GLB models from metaverse projects, JSON-based for API-friendly access . Best for developers building asset browsers needing structured CC0 data.

- **os3a-gallery** — Curated discovery platform for freely available GLB format 3D assets, built with Next.js and Three.js . Best for browsing and discovering CC0 GLB models.

- **forge-asset-libraries** — Rust libraries for agents and applications creating, validating, texturing, and consuming 3D assets . Best for developers building automated 3D asset pipelines.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- **Licensing varies dramatically**: Poly Haven assets are CC0 (no restrictions) ; BlenderKit's free tier requires attribution or subscription for full access ; marketplace assets carry per-purchase licenses. Always verify license terms before commercial use.

- **"Free" marketplaces still have costs**: TurboSquid, CGTrader, and Sketchfab charge per-asset or take seller commissions. Self-hosted platforms (3D RGB, 3Dēx) shift costs to infrastructure and maintenance .

- **Open-source marketplace platforms are early-stage**: 3D RGB and 3Dēx are functional but have roadmaps with pending features (payment gateways, multi-vendor support) . Evaluate completeness before committing to production.

- **Poly Haven is donation-funded**: its sustainability depends on Patreon and sponsors—consider supporting if you rely on it .
